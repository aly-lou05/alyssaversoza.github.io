Project 01 · Social Media Strategy

Instagram Social Media Growth

Objective

  • Grow Shark Group's Instagram following, leadership's priority platform, alongside other channels
  • Increase views and engagement on every post

Challenge

Net new followers / month 30–50 Slow and inconsistent
Views per post 500–1,000
Interactions per post 20–50

Content was barely reaching the target audience.

Strategy

I proposed and launched a new Q4 social media strategy with:

  • SMART goals
  • KPIs
  • Audience personas
  • Competitor research
  • Content themes

  1. Follower growth

    I built audience personas covering demographics, behaviors, preferences, and pain points, and researched which platforms each customer segment and location uses.

  2. Views and engagement

    I developed content pillars based on persona insights and industry trends, replicated past top-performing formats, and analyzed performance patterns to identify the best days and times to post each content type.

  3. Automated social media dashboard

    An AI-assisted metrics and content planner with automated routines that pull content from an app daily and refresh trending hashtags weekly. It also evaluates metrics and gives insights and recommendations.

Results

October became the strongest month in 90 days.

Total views 118,025 ▲ Up 259.8% vs Sept
Viewers (reach) 74,801 ▲ Up 498.5% vs Sept
Interactions 1,360 ▲ Up 169.8% vs Sept
Visitor to follower 8.61% ▲ Up 3.21 pts vs Sept

Recommendations

  • Test new hook formats to reduce skip rates
  • Create more value-led content that prompts conversation and lifts engagement

Project 02 · Meta Ads

Meta Ad Campaigns

Objective

  • Attract qualified tradespeople through a paid Meta Ads campaign.

Challenge

Cost per lead, week 1 $71.41 Well above the $20 target
Leads, week 1 2 Very few leads
$0Target $20$80

Strategy

  1. Simplified the conversion path

    I switched the conversion location from website plus Instant Forms to Instant Forms only, after finding the application form hard to reach once visitors arrived on the landing page.

  2. Refined targeting

    I broadened the audience size while defining more detailed targeting.

  3. Doubled down on what worked

    I revised the creatives and replicated the best performers from week one. Reels led on click-through rate and lead volume.

  4. Added an incentive

    I included an information package to encourage applications.

Results

After the relaunch, cost per lead dropped 82% and click-through rate rose.

Cost per lead $71.41→$12.57 ▼ Down 82% · within the $20 target
Click-through rate (all) 1.82%→2.36% ▲ Up 0.54 pts
Leads 2→9 Meta Instant Form
Spend $142.82→$113.17 −20.8% · 6 active ads (was 5)

Recommendations

  • Create a dedicated careers page where candidates can browse open roles.
  • Improve the page layout so forms load quickly and are easy to find as soon as visitors land.

Project 03 · Campaign

One-Week Giveaway Campaign, Built in a Day

Objective

  • Boost orders from partners in the final week of the month with a week-long giveaway campaign.

Challenge

Deadline Same day
Campaign run 1 week

Everything had to be ready to run for the rest of the week:

  • Social media post
  • Email campaign
  • Lead intake flow

Strategy

  1. Content across channels

    Designed the giveaway post, daily Instagram stories, and a Mailchimp email campaign directing partners to claim through the post.

  2. Lead intake flow

    Built a lead intake flow so every claim was matched to a work order number.

  3. Boost and engage

    Boosted the post to the property manager audience and replied to comments and DMs within the hour throughout the week.

Results

Partner responses 43 Each quoting a work order number
  • The campaign ran during the month's strongest week for work orders.
  • Leadership adopted the giveaway as a monthly campaign.
  • I developed a repeatable playbook for future launches.
